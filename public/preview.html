Art direction

  • Stylized 3D-rendered look with rounded, sculpted shapes.
  • Fixed isometric camera on a 2:1 diamond ground grid.
  • Vibrant, warm, saturated countryside palette.
  • Soft lighting from the upper-left with gentle ambient occlusion.
  • Friendly, slightly exaggerated proportions for mobile readability.
  • Clear silhouettes, clean edges and subtle contact shadows.
  • No scenery, sky, painted backgrounds, watermarks or text.
  • Original designs — not replicas of any existing game's artwork.
